      How it is tested. Drawings are split into development and test by a hash of their id (20 % test), so no drawing used during development is scored. Ours answers every test question; the other models answered a sample drawn across every source and question type, re-weighted to the test mix. Counts must be exact; areas, lengths and volumes within 2 %; “not sure” counts as wrong; free-text answers are judged by an LLM. Brackets are 95 % intervals.
