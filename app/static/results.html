Accuracy on the held-out test split (split by drawing, so no drawing seen during development is scored). Ours and the prior answer all 25,420 test questions; the frontier models were asked 400 questions sampled across every source and type, re-weighted to the test mix (so each source counts as much as it does in the test split). Counts must be exact; areas, lengths and volumes within 2 %; an abstention counts as wrong; IFC-bench free text is judged. The brackets are 95 % intervals.
