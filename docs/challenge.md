# Challenge brief (from the Junction platform)

## 16. Make Blueprints Talk (Stoa)

**Task:** A program that answers natural-language questions about **construction drawings and BIM models**, accurately and verifiably. **Show where in the drawing the answer came from** so a human can verify it in seconds.

- General models fail on complex technical drawings. They want approaches next year's frontier models won't make obsolete: hybrids of frontier models + CV + 3D/geometry + small fine-tuned models.
- Dataset of real drawings in many formats and languages, with example questions:
  - Preview (0.5 GB): https://fwjydgxpttajiqpfmqtj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/stoa-junction-2026/junction-dataset-preview.zip
  - Full (10 GB): https://fwjydgxpttajiqpfmqtj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/stoa-junction-2026/junction-dataset.zip
- Stoa: Helsinki "AI-native OS for construction", backed by Lifeline Ventures and the founders of Wolt, Supercell and Silo AI; Finland's largest pre-seed.
- **Judging (25% each):** Accuracy · Visualising reasoning for verification · Creativity · **Low inference cost**.
- **Prizes:** €1,000 + work trial at Stoa / €750 / €250.
