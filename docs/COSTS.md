Frontier rows are a 400-question stratified sample of the same test split; their $/1k is the raw sample mean. The sample over-represents the costly IFC and free-text cells, so re-weighted to the true test mix (docs/RESULTS.md, the figures the README quotes) they are much lower: e.g. $25.76 instead of $61.31 for Sonnet + sibling files. Our numeric answers make no paid calls; only the 110 free-text IFC questions call Claude Haiku. Local compute (CAD/IFC parsing on CPU, the YOLO detector on one GPU, ~0.17 s per drawing with flip test-time augmentation) prices at roughly $0.006 per 1,000 questions at $0.50 per GPU-hour.
